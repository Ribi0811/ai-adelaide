The audit task protects trust in an existing sales entry point; it is not demand generation or a measured ranking improvement. Search diagnosis must lead to a clear decision and targeted work, not an indefinite reporting loop. The existing prospect consent holds remain in place; do not manufacture outreach activity or count researched businesses as interested buyers.
